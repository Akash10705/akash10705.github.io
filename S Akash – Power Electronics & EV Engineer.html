IEEE 13-Bus Distribution System & Solar PV Integration

Power Systems
Smart grid

A study of an unbalanced feeder over 24 hours, with and without capacitors and solar PV.

  • Analyzed the IEEE 13-bus unbalanced feeder: phase-wise voltages and P/Q losses.
  • Modeled residential, agricultural, commercial and motor loads with 24-hour profiles.
  • Found weak buses from voltage profiles, feeder topology, line impedance and load distribution.
  • Evaluated shunt capacitors for Q-support, voltage regulation, power factor and feeder losses.
  • Integrated solar PV using irradiance and load profiles, and measured the 24-hour voltage improvement and loss reduction.
  • Compared base, load-varied, capacitor and PV cases.
  • Next: optimize PV and capacitor size and placement, add BESS and demand response.
